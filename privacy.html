Sketched History – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sketched History ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Sketched History Publisher app (the "App").

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by Sketched History to manage its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's TikTok account.

2. Information we access

When the Sketched History TikTok account authorises the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App may access:

  • basic account information (such as display name, avatar and account identifier);
  • the account's list of published videos; and
  • performance statistics for those videos (such as views, likes, comments and shares).

The App also uploads and publishes videos created by Sketched History through TikTok's Content Posting API.

3. How we use it

We use this information only to publish Sketched History's own content and to measure how that content performs. We do not use it for advertising profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or trade any data. Information is shared only with:

  • TikTok, as needed to publish content and retrieve account information; and
  • Composio, the third-party integration service that connects the App to TikTok. Composio stores the access tokens needed to keep the connection working, under its own privacy and security terms.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored by Composio. We take reasonable steps to keep account access secure and limit it to what the App needs. Statistics may be kept in our own records to track content performance.

6. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, or equivalent). Once access is revoked, the App can no longer read from or post to the account. You can also email us to ask that any stored data be deleted.

7. TikTok

TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to use of the TikTok platform. The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

8. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Northern Territory, Australia, and we aim to handle information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this address, with the date of the latest update shown above.
